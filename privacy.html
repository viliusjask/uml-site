Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

Unfuck My Life is a personal app used by its owner only. It is not offered to the public.

What the app stores

The app stores the owner's notes, tasks, reminders and related data on the owner's own phone and PC.

Google Drive

The app asks for the Google Drive scope drive.file. With it, the app can only see and change files that the app itself created. It cannot read any other file in the owner's Drive. The app uses these files to sync data between the owner's own devices.

AI model providers

Some features send data to an AI model service chosen by the owner, currently OpenRouter, which passes each request to a model provider such as OpenAI, Anthropic or Google. These features sort and title notes, extract facts from notes, answer questions in the assistant chat, estimate meals from text or photos, and suggest recipes. Depending on the feature, a request can contain note text, stored facts about the owner, chat history, meal descriptions and meal photos. These services process the data under their own privacy terms and may keep it for a limited time.

Sharing

There is no server run by the app. Apart from Google Drive, used for sync, and the AI model services above, data is not sent to anyone. It is never sold or used for advertising.

Contact

Questions go to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen of this app.
