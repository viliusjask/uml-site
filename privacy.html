Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

Unfuck My Life is a personal app used by its owner only. It is not offered to the public.

What the app stores

The app stores the owner's notes, tasks, reminders and related data on the owner's own phone and PC.

Google Drive

The app asks for the Google Drive scope drive.file. With it, the app can only see and change files that the app itself created. It cannot read any other file in the owner's Drive. The app uses these files to sync data between the owner's own devices.

Other services

Some features send the text of a note to an AI model provider chosen by the owner, to sort notes or answer questions. Only the text needed for that request is sent.

Sharing

There is no server run by the app. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Contact

Questions go to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen of this app.
